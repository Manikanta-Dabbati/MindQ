# MindQ Product Definition

## Problem Statement

Learners frequently work with static study resources such as PDFs, DOCX files and personal notes. Turning those resources into meaningful practice questions manually requires time and effort. Learners also need a way to assess their understanding and retain the results of their practice.

MindQ addresses this by connecting knowledge storage, AI question generation, assessment and progress tracking in a single workflow.

## Proposed Solution

MindQ converts user-provided study content into configurable MCQ practice. Users can upload documents, enter a topic or select existing Knowledge Vault materials, configure difficulty and question count, generate questions with AI, take quizzes, review results and track progress.

## Objectives

- Automate quality MCQ generation.
- Provide structured self-assessment.
- Store learning materials centrally.
- Track quiz performance.
- Provide actionable learning feedback.
- Support secure account management.
- Support free and paid product tiers.
- Provide a deployable SaaS foundation.
